Cybersecurity & IT Risk NIST SP 800-30 Assessment

Healthcare Sector Cyber Risk Assessment

Threat Vector Analysis, Historical Breach Data & Defense-in-Depth Strategy

This is a one-slider that sums up a series of projects completed for Professor Burns ISDS 4096: Cyber-risk Management Course. This comprehensive cybersecurity risk assessment analyzed threat surfaces and vulnerabilities across the medical sector under the NIST SP 800-30 framework. Evaluated historical healthcare breaches, modeled incident frequency trends, and created specific mitigations.

  • Framework: NIST SP 800-30 methodology evaluating threat likelihood and medical impact
  • Breach Investigation: Analyzed Anthem Inc. (~80M records affected), LSU Health New Orleans, and Walgreens
  • Threat Locations: Identified Network Servers, Email Spear-Phishing, and Stolen Hardware
  • Defense-in-Depth: MFA, IDS/IPS intrusion prevention, third-party vendor audits, and end-to-end data encryption

Database Architecture & SQL The Big 5 • Technical Report

Nordstrom Enterprise Database & SQL Analytics

Relational ERD, Normalized 3NF Schema & 10-Query Business Intelligence Suite

This was a in-depth project completed for Professor Ahemd's ISDS 3110 Database Class. End-to-end relational database engineered for Nordstrom Rack (~300 nationwide retail locations). Co-developed with "The Big 5" team at LSU, encompassing conceptual ER modeling, 3NF schema normalization, full MySQL DDL creation scripts, and complex analytical queries.

  • Architecture: Complete ER Diagram & Relational Schema across 10 business entities
  • SQL Engineering: DDL scripts (CREATE TABLE, FK constraints), data dictionary, and seed datasets
  • Analytical Matrix: 10 SQL queries with multi-table JOINs, Subqueries, HAVING clauses, and cost audits
  • Team Project: Co-created with Kayla Goudy, Isaac Joyner, Nicholas Mwenye, and Sarah Reid
